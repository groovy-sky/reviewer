This week featured 2 updates from Microsoft Blogs.

The updates highlight the importance of integrating AI with business outcomes, workforce readiness, and continuous governance to achieve measurable value. They emphasize practices such as broad AI deployment, unified AI investment management, and strong governance, which are linked to higher ROI and customer success. A notable exception is the absence of any updates addressing AI ethics or regulatory compliance.

Detailed list:

* [Scaling AI value without losing control: Lessons from Frontier Firms](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/11/12/scaling-ai-value-without-losing-control-lessons-from-frontier-firms/). - The IDC research, commissioned by Microsoft, surveyed 5,200 senior IT and business leaders across 28 industries to identify practices associated with high AI value. It found that organizations in the top 10% of AI maturity (Frontier Firms) achieve higher returns on AI investment (average 4x vs. 2.7x for low adopters) and report greater confidence in measuring AI value. Frontier Firms manage AI investments, models, agents, governance, and skills as a unified discipline. Key practices include exceeding planned AI spend, deploying AI broadly, scaling agent use, establishing governance processes, and implementing organization-wide AI skilling strategies. The research emphasizes that scaling AI value requires integrating technology with business outcomes, workforce readiness, and continuous governance.
* [From AI ambition to business impact](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/11/12/from-ai-ambition-to-business-impact/). - Microsoft Frontier Company has completed over 500 AI projects with 200+ customers across 28 countries, including Unilever, 3M, Northern Trust, and others. The company focuses on delivering measurable business value through AI by redesigning workflows, integrating technology with human expertise, and building trust and governance. Projects involve AI agents, digital twins, and automated processes that improve efficiency, reduce manual effort, and enable faster decision-making. Success is measured by customer outcomes, not just technology delivery.
