This week featured 1 update from Microsoft Devblogs.

AI coding agents may misinterpret SemVer labels, affecting upgrade risk assessment and merge outcomes.

Detailed list:

* [Do AI coding agents care about SemVer?](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/blog/do-ai-coding-agents-care-about-semver/). AI coding agents may interpret SemVer labels to adjust how thoroughly they review upgrades, with some models treating major releases as higher risk and skipping checks for patches, potentially leading to unchecked merges. This behavior varies by model and may affect outcomes, especially when updates are labeled incorrectly.
